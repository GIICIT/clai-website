Rosacea & Sensitive Skin Types



What Is Rosacea?

Rosacea is a chronic inflammatory skin condition that commonly appears on the cheeks, nose, forehead, or chin. It often presents as persistent redness, flushing, visible capillaries and in some cases, bumps or pustules that resemble acne. While the cause of rosacea isn’t fully understood, it’s often triggered by stress, heat, sun exposure, spicy food or certain skincare products



Understanding Sensitive Skin

Sensitive skin is a reactive skin type that can feel tight, stinging, itchy or red, especially when exposed to environmental changes or certain ingredients. It often goes hand in hand with rosacea and requires gentle, targeted care to keep it calm and balanced.

Types of Rosacea We Commonly Treat

Persistent redness with visible capillaries.

Redness with bumps and breakouts.

Skin becomes thickened or textured, often around the nose.

Irritation and redness around the eyes.
