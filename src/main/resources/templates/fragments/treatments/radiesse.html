Radiesse is an advanced injectable treatment that combines immediate structural support with long-term collagen stimulation. Formulated with calcium hydroxylapatite (CaHA), it helps stimulate your skin’s natural collagen and elastin production, improving firmness, elasticity and definition over time. Treatment is tailored to your individual anatomy and aesthetic goals.
