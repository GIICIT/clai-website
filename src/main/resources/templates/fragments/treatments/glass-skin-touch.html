The CLAI Glass Skin Touch is an advanced combination treatment designed to improve skin tone, texture, firmness and overall skin quality.

This treatment combines a carefully selected superficial chemical peel, targeted Morpheus8 and superficial microneedling with PRP, allowing us to work at different levels of the skin within one treatment.

It is particularly suited to skin where we want to focus on uneven tone, texture and overall rejuvenation.

The Treatment

RESURFACE — Professional Peel
We begin with a carefully selected superficial chemical peel to gently refresh the skin and encourage a brighter, smoother complexion.

TIGHTEN — Morpheus8
Morpheus8 is used strategically, focusing predominantly on the lower face where tightening and collagen remodelling are needed most.

Radiofrequency energy is delivered into the deeper layers of the skin to stimulate collagen and improve firmness, definition and skin laxity.

RENEW — Superficial Microneedling + PRP
We finish with superficial microneedling combined with your own Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP).

This final step focuses on overall skin quality, helping to support natural regeneration while refining texture and creating a healthier, more luminous appearance.

Who Is Glass Skin Touch For?
Ideal for concerns including:

  • Dullness
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Uneven texture
  • Enlarged pores
  • Fine lines
  • Loss of radiance
