Sunekos is an injectable skin regeneration treatment designed to improve skin quality, hydration, elasticity and fine lines while maintaining a completely natural appearance.

Rather than adding volume or changing facial contours, Sunekos works within the skin to support its natural extracellular matrix and improve the quality of the tissue itself.

How Does Sunekos Work?
Sunekos combines hyaluronic acid with a specific blend of amino acids.

This combination is designed to support fibroblast activity and the production of important structural components within the skin, including collagen and elastin.

The result is gradual improvement in the skin's hydration, firmness, elasticity and overall quality.

What Can Sunekos Help With?
Sunekos is particularly suited to:

  • Fine lines
  • Crepey skin
  • Dehydration
  • Loss of elasticity
  • Dull skin
  • Early signs of ageing
  • Poor skin quality
Where Can Sunekos Be Used?
One of the benefits of Sunekos is its versatility. It can be used on areas where improving skin quality is the priority, including the:
Face, Under-eye area, neck, décolletage, and Hands

It is particularly popular around the delicate eye area, where traditional volumising treatments may not always be appropriate.

Sunekos Is Not a Filler
Sunekos is not designed to sculpt the face or create significant volume.

Instead, the focus is on biostimulation and skin regeneration — helping your skin look healthier, smoother, more hydrated and naturally refreshed.

What to Expect
Sunekos is generally performed as a course of treatments, with the number and intervals determined according to your skin, age and individual concerns.

Improvements develop gradually as the skin responds to treatment.

The goal is not to make you look different.
