The O-Shot + G-Shot is an intimate rejuvenation treatment using Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) prepared from your own blood.

Designed to support intimate tissue quality and sensitivity, the treatment combines targeted PRF placement in both the O-Shot and G-Shot areas for a more comprehensive approach to female intimate wellness.

What is PRF?
PRF is a concentrated component of your own blood containing platelets, fibrin and naturally occurring growth factors.

Once prepared, it forms a fibrin-rich matrix that allows growth factors to be released gradually within the treated tissue, supporting the body's natural repair and regenerative processes.

O-Shot = Regeneration & Sensitivity
PRF is carefully placed into specific areas of the external and internal intimate tissue.

The treatment is designed to support tissue quality, circulation and sensitivity, particularly where women have noticed changes following childbirth, hormonal changes or ageing.

G-Shot = Targeted Enhancement
PRF is placed into the G-area of the anterior vaginal wall, creating a subtle increase in tissue volume while also providing the regenerative benefits of PRF.

The aim is to increase prominence and awareness of this area, which may enhance sensation for some women.

Why Combine the O-Shot + G-Shot?
Combining both techniques allows us to address intimate rejuvenation from two perspectives:

  • O-Shot → tissue regeneration & sensitivity
  • G-Shot → targeted enhancement & regenerative support
Who May Consider This Treatment?
The O-Shot + G-Shot may be considered by women experiencing changes associated with:
  • Ageing
  • Hormonal changes
  • Postpartum changes
  • Reduced intimate sensitivity
  • Changes in sexual experience
  • Changes in intimate tissue quality
A Treatment Using Your Own Biology
Because PRF is prepared entirely from your own blood, the treatment works with your body's own biological components rather than introducing a synthetic regenerative product.

Every treatment begins with a private consultation to discuss your concerns, medical history and expectations and to determine whether PRF intimate rejuvenation is appropriate for you.

Individual responses vary. PRF intimate rejuvenation is an emerging treatment and improvements in sexual function, orgasm, sensation or urinary symptoms cannot be guaranteed.
