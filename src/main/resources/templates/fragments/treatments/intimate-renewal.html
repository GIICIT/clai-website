The Intimate Renewal is our comprehensive intimate rejuvenation treatment, combining PRF O-Shot + G-Shot with Intimate Filler in one personalised treatment.

Designed for women experiencing changes following ageing, hormonal shifts, pregnancy, childbirth or changes in intimate tissue, the treatment takes a combined approach to both internal regenerative support and external volume restoration.

One Treatment. Two Approaches.

PRF O-Shot + G-Shot — Regenerate
Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood and carefully placed into targeted intimate areas.

Rich in platelets, fibrin and naturally occurring growth factors, PRF is used to support the body's natural regenerative processes and intimate tissue quality.

The combined O-Shot + G-Shot approach focuses on regenerative support, tissue quality and sensitivity, with targeted PRF placement according to your individual anatomy and concerns.

Intimate Filler — Restore
A carefully selected hyaluronic acid filler is placed into the labia majora to restore volume, softness and hydration to the external intimate area.

This can help address volume loss or deflation that may occur with hormonal changes, ageing, pregnancy, childbirth or changes in body composition.

Why Combine Them?
Intimate changes rarely involve only one aspect of the tissue.

By combining PRF regeneration with hyaluronic acid volume restoration, the CLAI Intimate Renewal allows us to take a more comprehensive approach:

  • REGENERATE → PRF tissue support
  • RESTORE → Volume & external cushioning
  • REHYDRATE → Improved softness and hydration
  • RENEW → Overall intimate rejuvenation
Who Is It For?
The CLAI Intimate Renewal may be considered by women who have noticed:
  • Changes after childbirth
  • Hormonal or age-related changes
  • Reduced intimate tissue quality
  • Changes in sensitivity
  • Loss of labia majora volume
  • Deflation
  • External tissue dryness
  • Changes in the appearance of the intimate area
Intimate Rejuvenation is Personalised to You
Every woman's anatomy, concerns and experience are different.

The CLAI Intimate Renewal is therefore not a one-size-fits-all treatment. Your treatment begins with a private consultation where we assess your concerns, discuss your expectations and determine the most appropriate PRF placement and filler treatment for you.

Our focus is on natural restoration, regenerative support and maintaining normal intimate anatomy, not creating an artificial or overfilled result.

Individual results vary. PRF intimate rejuvenation is an emerging treatment, and improvements in sexual function, orgasm or sensation cannot be guaranteed. All injectable procedures carry potential risks, which will be discussed during your consultation.
