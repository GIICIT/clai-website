Dermal filler

Our range of dermal filler treatments is designed to restore lost volume, enhance natural features, and create a more balanced, youthful appearance. Each treatment is tailored to your individual facial structure for results that look natural and refined.

Our dermal filler injections start at R4,280 per vial. A slight reduction in price applies when more than one vial is used during the same appointment, as some patients may require 2–3 vials for optimal results.

Available Treatments

Cheek fillers restore volume and lift to the mid-face, enhancing cheekbone definition and rejuvenating your overall facial profile.

By adding structure and contour, chin and jawline fillers create definition, improve facial balance, and sculpt a sharper, more harmonious profile.

Hands often show signs of aging first. Hand fillers restore volume, smooth crepey skin, and reduce the visibility of veins and tendons for a more youthful look.

The liquid facelift uses a combination of dermal fillers to lift, contour, and rejuvenate multiple areas of the face without surgery, providing a refreshed and youthful appearance.

Filling the lines that run downward from the corners of the mouth softens a sad or tired look, restoring a lifted and more positive expression.

These fillers smooth the deep folds between the nose and mouth, softening shadows and restoring a youthful contour to the mid-face.

Neck fillers improve skin texture, hydration, and elasticity while reducing lines and creases for a smoother, more refined neck appearance.

This advanced filler technique reshapes and refines the nose without surgery. It can smooth bumps, lift the nasal tip, and improve overall profile balance.

Tear trough fillers address hollowness and dark circles under the eyes, refreshing the eye area and reducing a tired appearance.

Temple fillers restore volume loss in the upper face, softening hollowing and creating a more balanced, youthful frame for the eyes.
