Hyperpigmentation

Hyperpigmentation refers to areas of the skin that appear darker than the surrounding skin. This happens when excess melanin - the pigment that gives skin its colour - is produced. It can become harmful and it can affect your confidence and skin appearance.

At CLAI, we understand how frustrating uneven skin tone and dark spots can be. Hyperpigmentation is a common skin concern that affects people of all skin tones and ages; however, the good news is that it’s highly treatable with the right approach.

Whether your pigmentation is caused by sun exposure, hormones or past acne breakouts, our team offers personalised treatments using the latest in medical aesthetics to help you achieve clear, radiant skin.

Types of Hyperpigmentation

Dark marks left behind after acne, eczema or skin injuries.

Often triggered by hormones (pregnancy, birth control or sun exposure). Here, melasma appears as symmetrical brown or grey-brown patches, typically on the face.

Flat brown spots that develop from years of sun exposure are commonly seen on the face, chest and hands.

Genetically driven, often darkening with sun exposure.

Can result from certain medications or chemical exposure.
