Hair Treatments

Our advanced PRP scalp treatment combines the regenerative power of Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) and Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) with a multi-level delivery approach for optimal results. The result is a comprehensive, science-driven treatment designed to strengthen, restore, and support healthier, fuller hair growth over time.
