Anti-Ageing

At CLAI we believe ageing is a natural and continuous process that cannot - and should not - be stopped. Our approach is not about fighting ageing, but about supporting healthy ageing. Through advanced treatments and a holistic understanding of skin health, we aim to maintain skin quality, support collagen production and help the skin age in the healthiest and most balanced way possible.

The Glogau Photoaging Scale

In aesthetic medicine, one of the most commonly used ways to understand skin ageing is the Glogau Photoaging Scale. This scale helps practitioners assess how the skin changes over time due to natural ageing and sun exposure.

By identifying the stage of skin ageing, we can better choose treatments that support healthy skin ageing, improve skin quality and maintain long-term skin health.

Skin shows very minimal signs of ageing. There may be mild sun damage, slight pigmentation or early dehydration, but wrinkles are usually not visible.

Treatment focus: prevention, skin health and collagen support.

Fine lines begin to appear, especially with facial movement. Pigmentation and early sun damage may become more noticeable and skin elasticity may start to decrease.

Treatment focus: collagen stimulation, skin rejuvenation and correcting early damage.

Wrinkles may become visible even when the face is at rest. Skin texture can change, pigmentation may increase and collagen loss becomes more evident.

Treatment focus: restoring skin quality, improving skin structure and supporting regeneration.

Deep wrinkles are present and skin may show significant sun damage and loss of elasticity.

Treatment focus: advanced skin rejuvenation and regenerative treatments to improve overall skin health.
