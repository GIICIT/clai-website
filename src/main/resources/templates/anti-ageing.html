Anti-Ageing

At CLAI we believe ageing is a natural and continuous process that cannot - and should not - be stopped. Our approach is not about fighting ageing, but about supporting healthy ageing. Through advanced treatments and a holistic understanding of skin health, we aim to maintain skin quality, support collagen production and help the skin age in the healthiest and most balanced way possible.

The Glogau Photoaging Scale

In aesthetic medicine, one of the most commonly used ways to understand skin ageing is the Glogau Photoaging Scale. This scale helps practitioners assess how the skin changes over time due to natural ageing and sun exposure.

By identifying the stage of skin ageing, we can better choose treatments that support healthy skin ageing, improve skin quality and maintain long-term skin health.
