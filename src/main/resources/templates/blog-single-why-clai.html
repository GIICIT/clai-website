Acne & Acne Scarring
Acne is one of the most common skin conditions, affecting individuals of all ages. It can be triggered by hormones, genetics, lifestyle and stress, leading to persistent breakouts, inflammation and scarring. At CLAI, we take a comprehensive approach to acne treatment by:

  • Controlling excess oil production with medical-grade skincare and treatments like chemical peels.
  • Reducing active breakouts and inflammation with targeted treatments such as Lumecca IPL, LED therapy and personalised home care routines.
  • Minimising post-acne scars and uneven skin texture using collagen-stimulating procedures like microneedling, PRP/PRF therapy and mesotherapy.
  • Addressing internal factors such as gut health, nutrition and hormonal imbalances that contribute to persistent acne.
Pigmentation & Sun Damage
Hyperpigmentation and sun damage are common concerns, especially in those with prolonged sun exposure or hormonal changes (such as melasma). Pigmentation can manifest as:
  • Dark spots and age spots caused by UV exposure.
  • Melasma, a condition often linked to hormonal fluctuations.
  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) from acne or skin irritation.

Rosacea & Sensitive Skin
Rosacea and sensitivity are characterised by redness, flushing, inflammation and fragile skin that reacts easily to triggers. Many people with these conditions experience:

  • Persistent redness and visible blood vessels.
  • Burning, stinging, or irritation when using skincare products.
  • Flare-ups triggered by heat, spicy foods, alcohol, or stress.

Severe Sensitivity & Barrier Repair
For those with compromised skin barriers, the skin becomes weakened, dehydrated and more prone to irritation. This can result from:

  • Overuse of harsh skincare products.
  • Environmental damage (pollution, extreme weather).
  • Chronic skin conditions like eczema or dermatitis.

Aging & Skin Rejuvenation
As we age, collagen production slows down, leading to:

  • Fine lines and wrinkles.
  • Loss of firmness and elasticity.
  • Dullness and uneven skin texture.
