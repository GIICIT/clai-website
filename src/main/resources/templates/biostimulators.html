Biostimulators

Sculptra is a unique, collagen-stimulating injectable that works beneath the skin to gradually restore volume, improve skin quality, and enhance natural contours. Unlike traditional fillers, which provide instant volume, Sculptra encourages your body to rebuild its own collagen for results that develop subtly and last up to two years.

Our Sculptra treatments are priced from R7,900 per vial, with a discounted rate available when multiple vials are used during the same session.

Biostimulators Treatments

Facial Sculptra treatments are ideal for restoring volume in areas affected by aging, such as hollow cheeks, temples, and jawline. It smooths lines, improves skin thickness, and provides a refreshed, natural look that evolves over time.

Sculptra can also be used to improve skin quality and contour in areas such as the buttocks, thighs, arms, and décolletage. By stimulating collagen production, it enhances firmness, smooths crepey skin, and improves overall skin texture.
