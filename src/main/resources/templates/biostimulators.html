What Are Biostimulators

Biostimulators are advanced injectable treatments that stimulate your body’s natural collagen production, helping restore skin firmness, improve elasticity, and support a more youthful appearance over time.

As we age, collagen production naturally declines, contributing to changes in skin structure, firmness and facial volume. Biostimulators encourage the skin’s natural regenerative processes, gradually improving its underlying support and overall quality.

Unlike traditional dermal fillers, which primarily focus on adding volume and contour, biostimulators focus on stimulating collagen production for progressive, natural-looking results.

The result? Skin that looks firmer, smoother and better supported, with improvements that develop gradually over time.
