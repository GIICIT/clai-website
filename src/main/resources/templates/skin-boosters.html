Skin Boosters

Skin boosters are injectable treatments designed to improve the skin’s hydration, texture and overall quality from within. By delivering hydrating ingredients, such as hyaluronic acid, into the skin, they help support a smoother, more supple and radiant complexion.

At CLAI, every treatment is tailored to your skin’s needs and aesthetic goals, with a focus on natural-looking results, improved skin quality and a healthy, refreshed appearance.
