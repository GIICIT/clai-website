Acne & Acne Scarring



What is Acne?

Acne is a common inflammatory skin condition that occurs when pores become blocked by excess oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria. It most commonly affects the face, chest, back, and shoulders. Acne can present in varying degrees of severity, from mild congestion to deeper, more persistent breakouts.



What Causes Acne Scarring?

When deeper acne lesions disrupt the skin’s underlying structure, the healing process may result in permanent textural changes. These scars vary in depth and appearance and typically require advanced, targeted aesthetic treatments to restore smoother, healthier-looking skin.
