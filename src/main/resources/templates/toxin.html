Toxin Treatments

Microtox, also known as Baby Botox, uses tiny, precise doses of botulinum toxin to soften fine lines without creating a “frozen” look. This treatment is perfect for those who want subtle, natural-looking results while maintaining facial movement and expression.

Neurotoxin injections can provide effective relief for chronic migraines. By targeting specific muscles in the head and neck, the treatment relaxes tension and helps prevent migraine triggers. Many patients experience a noticeable reduction in the frequency and severity of their headaches.

One of the most popular uses of neurotoxins is to smooth wrinkles in the upper face, such as frown lines, forehead lines, and crow’s feet. By temporarily relaxing the targeted muscles, the skin looks smoother, softer, and more refreshed.

Bruxism, or teeth grinding and clenching, can cause jaw pain, headaches, and damage to the teeth. Neurotoxin injections relax the overactive jaw muscles, easing discomfort and protecting your smile while improving overall jawline comfort.

A “peach pit” or “orange peel” texture on the chin, as well as downturned mouth corners (caused by the DAO muscle), can create an aged or tired look. With targeted neurotoxin injections, we smooth chin dimpling and lift the corners of the mouth for a more refined, youthful appearance.

Excessive sweating can be uncomfortable and affect confidence. Neurotoxin injections block the signals that activate sweat glands, significantly reducing sweat production in areas like the underarms, hands, or feet. The result is long-lasting dryness and comfort.

The Lip Flip enhances the upper lip by relaxing the surrounding muscles with tiny neurotoxin injections. This causes the lip to subtly “flip” outward, creating the appearance of fuller, more defined lips-without adding volume or using fillers.

Over time, vertical neck bands and sagging skin can affect the jawline and neck contour. The Nefertiti Lift targets the platysma muscle with neurotoxin injections to relax tight bands, smooth the neck, and gently lift the jawline-restoring a more elegant profile.
