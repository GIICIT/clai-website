Acne & Acne Scarring



What is Acne?

Acne is a common inflammatory skin condition that occurs when pores become blocked by excess oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria. It most commonly affects the face, chest, back, and shoulders. Acne can present in varying degrees of severity, from mild congestion to deeper, more persistent breakouts.

Types Of Acne

Blackheads: Open pores filled with oil and debris that oxidise, giving a dark appearance.
Whiteheads: Closed, clogged pores that appear as small white or skin-coloured bumps.

Papules & Pustules: Red, inflamed bumps that may contain pus and tenderness.
Nodules & Cysts: Deeper, more painful lesions beneath the skin’s surface that often increase the risk of scarring.



What Causes Acne Scarring?

When deeper acne lesions disrupt the skin’s underlying structure, the healing process may result in permanent textural changes. These scars vary in depth and appearance and typically require advanced, targeted aesthetic treatments to restore smoother, healthier-looking skin.

Types of Acne Scars

Ice Pick Scars: Deep, narrow indentations.
Boxcar Scars: Broad depressions with defined edges.
Rolling Scars: Shallow, wave-like depressions that create uneven texture.
Hypertrophic & Keloid Scars: (Raised Scars) These scars develop due to excess collagen production during healing, resulting in thicker, raised areas of skin.
